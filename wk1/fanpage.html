Jay Chou

周杰倫

Since debuting in 2000, Jay Chou has shaped the sound of modern Mandopop by fusing classical piano training, R&B and hip-hop rhythm, and traditional Chinese instrumentation into a style fans and critics alike call "Zhongguo Feng," or Chinese wind.

Born Jan 18, 1979 · New Taipei City, Taiwan Active 2000 – present Roles Music, film, direction
